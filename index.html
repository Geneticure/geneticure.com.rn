The renal denervation opportunity

Renal denervation isn't weak.
Patient selection is.

RDN is a minimally invasive, FDA-approved and now-reimbursed procedure that lowers blood pressure. But it will not scale until the right patients are selected. Treat ten unselected patients and three see no benefit or get worse, and today there's no way to know who will respond.

7 see meaningful benefit.  3 see no benefit, or worsen.

The counterintuitive insight

Being selective about who you treat is the fastest path to treating the most patients.
